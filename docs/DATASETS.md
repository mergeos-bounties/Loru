## Ethics & Compliance Notes
1. **No redistribution**: Do not host copies of dataset media. Link to originals only.
2. **License compliance**: Verify usage rights (e.g., commercial vs. research-only).
3. **Attribution**: Cite authors in all derived work.
4. **Privacy**: Avoid models that inadvertently leak personal data from signers.
5. **Bias awareness**: Document dataset limitations (e.g., signer demographics, sign coverage).

> ⚠️ **Always review source licenses** before integration. This catalog is informational only.
